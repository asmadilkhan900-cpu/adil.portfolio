Congenital Right PUJ Obstruction & Laparoscopic Anderson-Hynes Pyeloplasty

Pathophysiology: Pelviureteric intrinsic aperistaltic segment vs. Lower pole crossing vessel

Initial Diagnostic
Hydronephrosis & Renal Caliectasis
Differential
Exclusion of Malignancy / Nodes
Intervention
Laparoscopic Pyeloplasty + DJ Stent

Following personal diagnostic investigation regarding lymphadenopathy versus congenital hydronephrosis, imaging confirmed an aperistaltic stenosis at the right ureteropelvic junction. The clinical objective was dismembered surgical reconstruction to restore unobstructed laminar urinary flow.

Key Surgical Mechanics (Anderson-Hynes):
1. Mobilization of right colon & exposure of dilated renal pelvis.
2. Excision of the narrow aperistaltic ureteral junction.
3. Lateral spatulation of the healthy ureter (1.5–2.0 cm).
4. Watertight, tension-free anastomosis with 4-0/5-0 Vicryl over a double-J stent.
